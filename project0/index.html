Becoming Friends with Your Camera

Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

From left to right: 0.5×, 1×, 2×, and 5×.

In the close-up photo, my face looks distorted because the camera is so close. As I move farther away and zoom in, my face looks flatter and more natural. The change in perspective comes from moving the camera, while zoom keeps my face about the same size.

Architectural Perspective Compression

I took these photos of Hilgard Hall while walking closer and zooming out. The 2× photo looks flatter, while the 0.5× photo makes the front of the building and the stairs stand out more. Moving closer makes the depth of the scene more noticeable.

The Dolly Zoom

I moved backward while zooming in so the teddy bear stayed about the same size in every photo. The bear stays in place, but everything around it seems to unwarp. Putting the photos together creates the dolly zoom effect.
